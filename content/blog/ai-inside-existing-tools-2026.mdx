You do not want to buy new software. You already pay for Gmail, WhatsApp, Google Workspace, Zoho, Tally, and probably a couple more. You want the AI to help you inside those tools.

Fair. Here is the honest 2026 state of AI inside every mainstream tool Indian businesses use, what is genuinely useful, what is demo-only, and where a small custom integration for under ₹1 lakh beats moving to a whole new platform.

## Google Workspace / Gmail (with Gemini)

Google added Gemini AI across Gmail, Docs, Sheets, and Meet.

### Genuinely useful in 2026:

- **Gmail "help me write"** for drafting replies. Save 30 seconds per email. Ships as decent drafts you edit rather than send blindly.
- **Meeting summaries in Google Meet.** Auto-generated notes are 85%+ accurate on standard business meetings. Worth turning on.
- **Docs "help me write"** for first drafts of standard docs (proposals, briefs, memos). Cuts blank-page paralysis.

### Demo-only, do not bother:

- **"Ask Gemini about your documents"** across Drive. Slow, misses obvious things, does not respect folder structure well. Try it, will not stick.
- **Sheets AI formulas.** Better than nothing but weaker than a competent analyst with normal formulas.
- **"Summarise this thread" in Gmail.** Works but rarely faster than just reading the thread.

### Cost:

Gemini for Google Workspace: ₹1,800/user/month. For a 10-person team, ₹18,000/month.

### Worth it?

For most Indian small teams, only worth it for 2-3 heavy users (the founder, sales lead, ops manager). Do not roll out to everyone by default.

## Microsoft 365 (with Copilot)

Microsoft added Copilot across Outlook, Word, Excel, Teams, PowerPoint.

### Genuinely useful:

- **Excel Copilot for data analysis.** Can build pivot tables, chart suggestions, formulas from natural language. Best-in-class for Excel.
- **PowerPoint slide generation** from a Word doc. Not great slides, but a decent starting point.
- **Teams meeting summaries and action items.**

### Demo-only:

- **Word Copilot for full documents.** First drafts are lifeless. Editing to make them sound like a human takes as long as writing from scratch.
- **Outlook Copilot for emails.** Similar to Gmail's version. OK for short replies, poor for anything nuanced.

### Cost:

Copilot for Microsoft 365: ₹2,400/user/month. Higher than Google Workspace equivalent.

### Worth it?

If your team lives in Excel doing heavy analysis, Copilot is the best value in the market for that specific use case. Otherwise, marginal.

## WhatsApp Business

WhatsApp has native AI reply suggestions in the business app.

### Genuinely useful:

- **Quick reply suggestions** for common questions when you have set up the templates.
- **Automatic labels** on incoming messages (order, complaint, inquiry).

### What is missing:

Everything actually useful (auto-reply, order lookups, AI-drafted replies) requires the WhatsApp Business Cloud API and a custom integration. See [Automate WhatsApp Replies With AI](/blog/automate-whatsapp-replies-with-ai-india) for details.

### Worth it?

Native WhatsApp AI in 2026 is minimal. Real AI on WhatsApp needs a custom build.

## Zoho (CRM, Books, Desk)

Zoho has "Zia," their AI, embedded across the suite.

### Genuinely useful:

- **Zia in Zoho Books** for auto-categorising expenses and matching payments. Real time saved for small businesses doing bookkeeping.
- **Zia in Zoho Desk** for suggesting canned replies to support tickets. Decent quality.
- **Anomaly detection** in Zoho CRM (leads that stopped responding, deals that stalled). Useful for larger sales teams.

### Demo-only:

- **Zia "AI assistant"** for freeform questions across your data. Slow and often wrong. Skip.
- **Zia writer** for content generation. Weak compared to GPT/Claude direct.

### Worth it?

If you already pay for Zoho, use the built-in AI features that touch your specific workflows. Do not upgrade specifically for Zia.

## Tally (accounting)

Tally added "TallyPrime AI" in 2026 for small business accounting.

### Genuinely useful:

- **Auto-classification** of transactions based on past patterns.
- **Anomaly alerts** for unusual entries (duplicate invoices, unusual amounts).

### Demo-only:

- **Natural language queries** ("show me sales in June for customer X"). Works but often slower than the traditional reports Tally already has.

### Cost:

Included in TallyPrime subscription for existing users.

### Worth it?

For most Tally users, the AI features are quiet quality-of-life improvements. Use them if you are already on Tally. Do not switch to Tally for the AI.

## The custom-integration option (under ₹1 lakh)

Here is where things get interesting. Instead of buying yet another SaaS AI tool, you can add a small custom AI layer on top of the tools you already have.

### The cheapest AI win for most small teams

**A custom Gmail assistant that drafts replies in your company's voice.**

- Reads incoming emails
- Drafts a reply using your past emails as style examples
- You review, tweak, hit send

Build cost: ₹60,000 – ₹1,20,000. Running cost: ₹1,500 – ₹4,000/month.

Why this beats Gmail's native "help me write":
- Learns YOUR tone, not generic corporate tone
- Uses YOUR past decisions ("we usually offer 15% discount to this customer type")
- Integrates with YOUR CRM ("this person's last order was 3 months ago, mention that")

Real time saved: 45-90 minutes per day for a founder or ops lead who does heavy email.

### Other under-₹1L wins

**Auto-summarise long WhatsApp business threads at end of day.**
Build cost ₹40,000-₹80,000. Sends you a daily digest of what happened across your WhatsApp channels. Useful if you have multiple team WhatsApp groups you cannot follow live.

**AI expense categoriser that reads your bank statement PDFs.**
Build cost ₹60,000-₹1,00,000. Reads your bank statement, categorises each transaction against your Tally chart of accounts, outputs a ready-to-import file. Saves your CA firm real hours.

**Slack/Teams command that queries your database in plain English.**
Build cost ₹80,000-₹1,50,000. Your team can type "how many orders yesterday" in Slack and get an answer. Removes the "I need to ask the developer" bottleneck.

### The pattern

The best custom AI additions in 2026 are small, focused, and integrated into where your team already works. They do not require your team to learn a new tool. They make an existing tool 20% smarter.

Cost: ₹50,000 – ₹1,50,000 for most useful ones.
Time to ship: 2-4 weeks.
Payback: usually within 6 months on time saved alone.

## What NOT to add

Do not add AI into your team's tools if:

- **The tool is being replaced in 6 months.** Do not invest in AI on top of software you are about to migrate away from.
- **Your team resists the tool already.** Adding AI does not make people love a tool they hate.
- **The AI would need access to sensitive data your compliance rules cannot allow.** Solve the compliance question first.
- **You cannot name the person who will use the AI feature daily.** Without a specific human owner, unused AI features become embarrassing sunk cost.

## FAQs

**Is Google Workspace AI (Gemini) worth the extra cost for a small team?**

Only for 2-3 heavy users. Do not roll out to the whole team by default. ₹1,800/user/month adds up fast.

**Can I add AI to Zoho or Tally without replacing them?**

Yes. Both support integrations. You can add a custom AI layer that reads from and writes to Zoho/Tally without leaving your existing setup.

**What is the smallest AI upgrade I can add to my team's Gmail this month?**

A custom reply drafter trained on your past emails. Build cost ₹60,000-₹1,20,000. Saves 45-90 minutes per day for heavy email users.

**Do I need Microsoft Copilot or is a custom thing cheaper?**

For pure Excel work, Copilot is best value. For everything else, a small custom integration usually beats Copilot at 1/5 the annual cost.

**Can AI read my WhatsApp Business chats and give me a daily summary?**

Yes, with a custom build. Requires the official WhatsApp Business Cloud API. Cost: ₹40,000-₹80,000 build, ₹1,500-₹3,000/month running.
