## The Idea

India's solar energy sector is booming, but finding a reliable solar installer is still a mess. Google searches return lead-gen farms with paid listings, not genuine directories. I saw a gap — a technical SEO play that could rank organically and actually be useful.

## Day 1: The Build

I registered the domain at 11 PM on a Friday. By Saturday evening, I had a working Next.js app with PostgreSQL, a basic search interface, and city-wise installer listings scraped from public government data. The entire backend was built with Claude Code — I described what I wanted, iterated on the schema, and let it generate the CRUD operations and API routes.

## Day 2: The Content

Sunday was all about content. I wrote unique city pages for 50+ Indian cities with proper heading hierarchies, local keywords, and structured data. Each page answered the exact questions people search for: "best solar installer in [city]", "solar panel cost in [city]", "government subsidies for solar in [city]".

The key insight: these are informational queries with clear commercial intent. Google rewards pages that answer the question directly, not pages that just list businesses.

## Day 3: The Launch

Monday morning I deployed to Vercel, submitted the sitemap to Google Search Console, and set up monitoring. The first 24 hours were quiet — zero impressions. Then on Day 4, Google started crawling aggressively.

## The Results

By Day 6, indexed pages went from 3 to 51 — a 17x increase. Organic impressions started flowing in by Day 8. The site was ranking on page 1 for several city-level keywords within two weeks.

## Lessons Learned

1. **Speed matters more than perfection.** Ship first, optimize later. Three days to a working product is better than three months to a perfect one.

2. **Claude Code changed my workflow.** I'm not a prompt engineer — I describe what I want in plain English and iterate. The code quality is good enough for a first version, and I refactor after.

3. **Technical SEO is a moat.** Most directory founders don't understand indexation, canonical tags, or structured data. This knowledge alone can give you a 6-month head start on competition.

4. **Content velocity wins.** 50+ unique city pages in one weekend. Most competitors had 10-15 generic pages. Google rewarded the depth and specificity.
