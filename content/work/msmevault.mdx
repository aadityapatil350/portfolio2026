## Context

India has hundreds of government schemes for Micro, Small, and Medium Enterprises (MSMEs), but the information is scattered across multiple government websites with poor searchability. Small business owners struggle to find schemes they're eligible for.

## Problem

No single, well-organized directory existed for MSME government schemes in India. The available information was fragmented across ministry websites, PDF documents, and regional offices — making it nearly impossible for a small business owner to discover relevant schemes.

## What I Built

MSMEVault.in — a clean, searchable directory of MSME government schemes organized by category (manufacturing, services, export, women entrepreneurs), state, and eligibility criteria. Each scheme page includes eligibility requirements, application process, benefit details, and direct links to apply.

The monetization strategy: partner with Non-Banking Financial Companies (NBFCs) that offer MSME loans. When users browse schemes and show intent to apply, they can also request loan quotes from partner NBFCs — generating qualified leads.

## Stack

- **Next.js** — Static generation for all scheme pages
- **Technical SEO** — Structured data, auto-sitemap, internal linking, unique meta tags per scheme

## Outcomes

- Live with 100+ scheme listings across 15 categories
- Organic traffic growing month-over-month through technical SEO
- NBFC lead generation pipeline established
- Zero paid marketing — 100% organic acquisition

## Lessons

Government data is public but poorly structured. The value isn't in the data itself — it's in organizing it, making it searchable, and connecting it to related services (like financing). Directories monetize best when they solve a search problem AND connect users to a transaction.
