## The business problem

Managing generation forecasts and schedules for a large renewable energy portfolio is not one problem. It is dozens of small, fast, high-stakes problems happening every fifteen minutes.

Every plant has to submit a fresh generation schedule to the grid on a strict window. Miss the window and the operator pays a penalty. Send the wrong number and the operator pays a penalty. Do this manually across 40+ plants and someone is going to miss a window.

The team was juggling manual portal logins, custom XML file uploads, and offline spreadsheets. Coverage was patchy. Every senior operator carried a mental map of which plant needed what and when. Onboarding a new operator took months.

## What was happening before

- Manual logins to multiple regulatory portals every fifteen minutes.
- Custom XML schedule files built by hand.
- Offline spreadsheets tracking which plant had submitted what.
- One missed window costing real money in penalties.
- Every senior operator irreplaceable, because the process lived in their head.

## What I built

A single operational platform that carries the whole 15-minute cycle end to end.

Forecasts flow in from multiple sources. Schedules are generated automatically. Regulatory submissions happen through direct portal connectors. Every submission is logged, timestamped, and traceable. Operators supervise. They do not do the work by hand any more.

Then a second layer of automation was added on top for daily reporting, deviation analysis, and compliance filings. All of it runs on the same shared data.

## The outcome

- **3,400+ MW of active capacity** managed through the platform.
- **40+ plants** running concurrently across multiple operators.
- **15-minute schedule engine** with 99.9% revision reliability.
- **Zero missed submission windows** since production launch.
- **Days-not-months onboarding** for new operators — the process lives in the software, not in someone&apos;s head.

## Why this matters to you

If your business depends on hitting a strict deadline every N minutes, hours, or days — order cutoffs, GST filings, tender submissions, insurance renewals, courier bookings — this is the same class of problem. High frequency. Strict windows. Penalties for lateness. Every senior person carrying it in their head.

The fix is not more people. It is a small piece of software that owns the cycle so your team can supervise instead of run.
